# ClipMoa Support

ClipMoa is an iPhone app that organizes the photos, screenshots, links and text you save by adding a title, summary and tags.
By default, summaries are made by the AI on the device (Apple Intelligence).

## Contact

- Email: [red@enfre.com](mailto:red@enfre.com)
- If something goes wrong, please include your device model, iOS version, and which screen you were on and what you did.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where are AI summaries made?

By default, by Apple Intelligence on the device. In Settings, you can pick one of Claude, Gemini or OpenAI and enter your own
API key to summarize with that company's AI. Before anything is sent, the app tells you what is sent to which company and asks
for your agreement.

### Does it work on devices that can't use Apple Intelligence?

Yes. Saving, reading text in images and search still work, and items are kept as "Saved without AI" without a summary.
Once AI becomes available, they are summarized then.

### Where are my saved items?

Only on your devices and in your iCloud. They sync through iCloud to your other iPhones that use the same Apple Account.
The developer runs no servers and cannot see your saved items.

### How do I delete an item?

Open the item and tap "Delete" at the bottom. It is deleted from the device and iCloud together.
